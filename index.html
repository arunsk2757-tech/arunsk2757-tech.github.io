How I work
with AI.

Every project runs the same five steps. I lead and decide; AI does the heavy lifting, fast.

  1. 01

    Brief

    MeLearn the business, the visitors and what the page must achieve.

    AIResearch, summarise, list the open questions.

  2. 02

    Direct

    MeChoose the direction and set the standard.

    AIGenerate layouts, copy and images to choose from.

  3. 03

    Build

    MePlan the structure and review every line.

    AIWrite the code, the visuals and the 3D.

  4. 04

    Review

    MeTest on real phones and laptops, detail by detail.

    AIFix and refine, version after version.

  5. 05

    Ship

    MeHand over, so the client's team can run it.

    AIDraft the guides and documentation.
